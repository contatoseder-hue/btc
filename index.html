TIMEFRAME STATUS

Each timeframe compares its latest candle OPEN with WMA 100 and WMA 200: above both = BULLISH, below both = BEARISH, and between the averages = NEUTRAL. The top Overall Status becomes BULLISH only when all eight timeframe opens are above their WMA 200, and BEARISH only when all eight are below. Otherwise it stays WAITING. The separate 15-minute Overall WMA Trend compares WMA 100 with WMA 200.

⚠️ TRADING CAUTION

If the overall market shows strong bearish conditions, be cautious with BUY signals. If the market shows strong bullish conditions, be cautious with SELL signals. Always confirm the broader trend before entering a trade.

⚠️ READ BEFORE TRADE

Do NOT buy at Market order. Wait for the ECI entry level and use a LIMIT order.

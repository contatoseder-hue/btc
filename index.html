Standard mode: ECI direction must match the 1-minute WMA trend, then a closed 1-minute rejection candle confirms the 15-minute swing level. Optional Paper Limit Entry mode skips the rejection-candle requirement and simulates a limit at the nearest confirmed support/resistance (or a nearby manual override); it is higher risk and expires after 30 minutes. Support/resistance stays fixed as price moves, changing only if crossed/invalidated or replaced by a newer confirmed, closer swing. Demo sizing assumes $10 risk at the initial stop and uses 10× leverage for estimated margin display only; position quantity and PnL remain risk-based, and liquidation is not modeled. USD PnL is an estimate and excludes fees, spread, slippage and instrument-specific contract sizing. TP1 is 2R; 50% exits there and the remaining 50% targets 3R, with the stop moved to entry after TP1. State, pending setup/order, levels, open position and trade history are saved locally and an active robot resumes on refresh. JavaScript cannot monitor trades while the browser/page is closed; it resumes only when the dashboard is open again. Quotes are polled about every 15 seconds, not tick-by-tick. Paper trading only; no exchange orders are sent. Custom assets are not enabled in this test.

TIMEFRAME STATUS

Each timeframe uses its latest CLOSED candle: close above WMA 100 and WMA 200 = BULLISH, below both = BEARISH, and between the averages = NEUTRAL. OVERALL STATUS becomes BULLISH or BEARISH only when all eight timeframe confirmations agree; otherwise it stays WAITING. Trend Status compares 1-minute WMA 100 with WMA 200 using closed candles.

⚠️ TRADING CAUTION

If the overall market shows strong bearish conditions, be cautious with BUY signals. If the market shows strong bullish conditions, be cautious with SELL signals. Always confirm the broader trend before entering a trade.

⚠️ READ BEFORE TRADE

Do NOT buy at Market order. Wait for the ECI entry level and use a LIMIT order.
